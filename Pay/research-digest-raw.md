## Направление: market-geo

### Резюме
The mobile puzzle genre is healthy and growing: Sensor Tower puts 2025 puzzle IAP revenue at over $10B (+14% YoY, second-grossing genre after strategy) on ~9.7B downloads, while AppMagic's narrower IAP-only measure shows $8.2B (+7.6%). Downloads and spending have opposite geographies: India (14-18% of global downloads), the US, Brazil and Indonesia dominate download volume, but consumer spending is concentrated in the US (~30% of worldwide game revenue), China (iOS-only, 15.7%) and Japan (12.1%), with Europe (UK/Germany/France) driving most of 2025's growth — puzzle specifically posted its strongest gains in Europe, and Royal Match earns 53% of its revenue in the US, 9% in Japan, 5% in the UK. iOS platform share amplifies this split: iOS captures 64.2% of global consumer app spend from only ~29% of devices, with high iOS penetration in Japan (68%), US (61%), Australia (56%), Canada (54%), UK (51%) versus marginal iOS share in India (4%), Indonesia (10%), Brazil (16%), Mexico (23%) and Turkey (24%). Within puzzle, growth is led by merge (+80% to $1.4B, 60% of genre growth), block puzzle (revenue ~10-12x YoY), and sort/screw puzzle (~2x each); tap-away is not tracked as a distinct subgenre and its leading titles are small (~10-12M lifetime downloads, low revenue), so Maze Arrows should benchmark against the block/sort hybrid-casual playbook. Hybrid-casual was the only segment to grow IAP revenue in 2025 (+20% to $4.2B), hybrid-casual puzzle runs roughly 50/50 ads-to-IAP, and rewarded-ad share of formats grew 53.9% — all validating the planned ads+subscription hybrid. Notably, puzzle players on iOS out-retain and out-convert Android (D30 retention 6.69% vs 4.14%; D30 spender share 7.82% vs 7.0%), supporting an iOS-first launch aimed at US/Japan/UK/DE/FR/AU/CA storefronts.

### Выводы для локалей/цен
Locale priority for an iOS-only ads+subscription puzzle game: (1) en-US is the core revenue market — ~30% of global game spend, 61% iOS share, 53% of Royal Match revenue; launch and price-anchor the subscription there (standard US casual price points, no discount). (2) Japan is the single biggest gap in the current 8 locales — 12.1% of global game spend, the highest iOS share of any major market (68%), and the #2 country for puzzle spend; adding ja localization should be the top new-locale investment. (3) Germany and France are also missing — Europe drove most of 2025's puzzle IAP growth and UK/DE/FR lead Western European spend with 32-51% iOS share; de and fr are the next two locales to add, while en base already covers UK/AU/CA (all 51-56% iOS, high ARPU). (4) The existing hi, en-IN, id, th locales are download/volume plays with near-zero iOS monetization potential (iOS share: India 4%, Indonesia 10%; Thailand similar tier) — keep them for organic reach and ad impressions, but set aggressive regional subscription discounts (App Store price tiers ~20-35% of US) or expect ads to be effectively the only revenue there; note India/Indonesia barely exist as iOS markets at all. (5) pt-BR reaches Brazil's #3 global download volume but only 16% iOS — ad-first market, steep regional pricing. (6) ru has decent iOS web share (~34%) but App Store payment restrictions in Russia cripple subscription billing — treat as ads-only. Monetization design: follow the hybrid-casual puzzle playbook (~50/50 ads-to-IAP): rewarded video as the ad backbone (format share +54% in 2025), interstitials capped for retention, and a subscription bundling no-ads + hints/energy value (casual precedent shows subscriptions can reach >12% of revenue); because tap-away has no proven deep-IAP economy, the subscription plus rewarded ads — not consumable IAP — should carry monetization, with weekly/monthly SKUs and a trial tested primarily in US/JP/UK/DE/FR/AU/CA storefronts.

## Направление: ad-ecpm

### Резюме
iOS ad monetization in 2025-2026 shows a steep geo pyramid: for casual games, iOS rewarded-video eCPM runs ~$12 in Europe/North America, ~$8-11 in Japan/Korea, HK/TW and Russia, ~$4.7-5.5 in LATAM and Southeast Asia, and only ~$2.8 in India (TopOn H1 2025); country-level tables (Appodeal Q4 2024, Tenjin/CAS 2024) put US iOS rewarded video at $19.6-24.4, with South Korea, Australia, Japan and UK in the $10-21 band. Interstitials monetize at roughly 70-85% of rewarded video, while banners are near-worthless on iOS (~$0.19 global casual average; ~$0.45-0.99 even in the US). The US alone generates 56% of global iOS in-game ad revenue (Tenjin Q1 2026), making tier-1 English markets the core of any ads-driven plan; notably Russia is the #3 iOS ad-revenue geo at 6%. ATT is the key iOS-specific drag: only ~35% of users opt in overall (~39% for gaming; board games ~30%), and untracked impressions are priced ~34% lower (trackable ones command +51%), which historically cut publisher ad revenue from iOS users by ~21%. Ad-ARPDAU benchmarks for casual/puzzle sit around $0.03-0.10 globally (puzzle ~$0.08, AppsFlyer 2025 forecast), with TopOn showing casual-game 30-day ad LTV in EU&NA/JPKR several times higher than emerging markets (<$0.08 LTV30 on Android in SEA/SA/LATAM). Standard stack for an indie iOS puzzle title in 2026 is mediation (AppLovin MAX, Unity LevelPlay, or AdMob mediation; TopOn as a niche alternative) with AppLovin, Mintegral, Unity Ads, Liftoff/Vungle and AdMob as the dominant iOS demand (AppLovin 39% share on iOS per Tenjin Q1 2026); Russia requires Mintegral/Yandex demand. Rewarded + interstitial together contribute ~84% of casual-game ad revenue, so the ad design (and the value of an ad-free subscription tier) should center on those two formats. Data recency caveat: full country-level eCPM tables are 2024 (Appodeal Q4 2024, Tenjin/CAS Q2 2024); 2025-2026 sources (TopOn H1 2025, Tenjin Q1 2026) publish region-level numbers and shares — both are provided; Turkey has no reliable public 2025 iOS eCPM table (proxy: tier-2, roughly $3-8 rewarded on iOS).

### Выводы для локалей/цен
Ad revenue potential ranks the storefronts sharply: (1) US/Canada/Australia/UK — iOS casual rewarded eCPM $12-24, interstitial $10-23, and the US alone is 56% of global iOS ad revenue; the existing en base locale covers all four, so these should be the primary launch/UA targets and the anchor for subscription pricing. (2) Japan and South Korea are the biggest gaps in the current 8-locale set: iOS rewarded $10-21 country-level, Japan is 11% of global iOS ad revenue, and casual-puzzle affinity is high — adding ja and ko is the highest-ROI localization move suggested by this data. Germany/France (EU&NA region, casual iOS RV ~$12, Germany fastest-growing eCPM in Q2 2025) are a similar, slightly smaller gap. (3) ru is a justified locale: Russia is the #3 iOS ad-revenue geo (6%) with mid-tier eCPM (RV $8.4-8.6), but monetization there requires Mintegral/Yandex demand in the mediation stack, and App Store subscription payments in RU are impaired — plan Russia as ads-mostly. (4) The already-localized emerging locales (hi/en-IN — India RV $2.80; id/th — SEA RV ~$5.5, INT ~$3.1; pt-BR — LATAM RV $4.7-6.2) deliver 4-7x less ad revenue per impression and <$0.08 30-day ad LTV; treat them as scale/chart-ranking markets, price the subscription at heavily discounted regional tiers (their ATT opt-in is higher — Brazil 50%, Turkey 42% — which partially offsets low eCPM), and lean on rewarded-heavy design there. Monetization design implications: banners are economically irrelevant on iOS ($0.19 casual average) — build around rewarded video + interstitial (~84% of casual ad revenue); use AppLovin MAX or AdMob as mediation with AppLovin, Mintegral, Unity Ads, Liftoff and AdMob demand (the 2026 standard for indie iOS puzzle games); set the ad-free subscription price with a floor equal to expected foregone ad ARPDAU by geo (~$0.03-0.10 for casual puzzle, top of range in US), i.e. a US monthly price can carry ~$2-3+/month of pure ad-replacement value while Indian/SEA tiers cannot; and invest in an ATT pre-prompt since only ~30-39% consent determines a large share of achievable iOS eCPM.

## Направление: sub-economics

### Резюме
Subscription economics on iOS in 2025-2026 are extremely geography-skewed: RevenueCat's State of Subscription Apps 2026 (115K apps, $16B revenue, 2025 data) shows median Day-60 revenue-per-install of $0.55 in North America vs $0.33 Western Europe vs $0.11 in India/Southeast Asia, and D35 download-to-paid of ~2.6-2.8% in NA vs 0.7-1.4% in IN/SEA — a 4-5x conversion gap and 5x revenue gap. Adapty's 2026 report ($3B revenue, 2025 data) confirms North America alone is ~55% of global subscription revenue and per-country 1-year LTV tops out in Switzerland/Qatar/Israel/UK/Japan ($23-28) with US at $19.9; India prices at 0.6x and Indonesia/Turkey 0.7x of the US index. Trial-to-paid medians run 34.2% (NA) down to 15.2% (IN/SEA) per RevenueCat; Adapty's global averages are install-to-trial 11.2% and trial-to-paid ~26-28%. Plan mix has shifted decisively to weekly: weekly plans now generate 55.6% of all app subscription revenue (Adapty, 2025 data), and a weekly plan with a 3-day trial is the highest-LTV configuration; but weekly Y1 retention is only 3.4% vs 17% monthly and 44.1% annual (RevenueCat 2025). Gaming-specific subscription data: 82% of gaming subscriptions sold are weekly, 73% of gaming trials are ≤4 days, gaming D60 RPI is $0.14 median, D35 download-to-paid 1.0%, trial-to-paid 25%, and the median gaming subscription app makes just $56/month a year after launch (top 10%: $4,554). Critically for the ru locale: Apple stopped processing ALL App Store payments in Russia on April 1, 2026, so Russian storefront subscription revenue is now zero. The implication for Maze Arrows: subscriptions will be paid for almost entirely by US/UK/CA/AU/WE storefronts; the already-localized hi/id/th/en-IN/pt-BR locales are ads-first markets needing ~40-50% lower regional prices; design the sub around weekly+3-day-trial with an annual anchor.

## Направление: apple-pricing

### Резюме
Apple's current pricing system (in force since Dec 2022, unchanged through 2026) gives every app 900 price points from $0.29 to $10,000 across 175 storefronts / ~44 currencies; you pick one base storefront and Apple auto-equalizes the other 174 using FX and tax data — critically, this equalization is NOT purchasing-power-based, so auto-generated prices in India/Brazil/Indonesia/Turkey are 2-3x higher than what top-grossing apps actually charge there, and manual per-storefront prices (which Apple never auto-adjusts) are mandatory for emerging markets. Apple is merchant of record almost everywhere: it calculates, collects and remits VAT/GST, and developer proceeds are computed as price minus tax, then minus commission — the developer never touches VAT for IAP/subscriptions. For ZimmaInc the effective commission is 15% on everything via the Small Business Program (<$1M prior-year proceeds), and even outside SBP subscriptions drop to 15% after a subscriber's first paid year. The 2025 Epic rulings let US apps link out to commission-free web checkout (9th Cir. affirmed Dec 2025 but remanded so Apple may later charge a "reasonable" fee), and EU DMA alternative terms (2% acquisition + 5-13% store services + 5% CTC) only matter if you opt out of standard IAP — for a small indie at 15% both are largely ignorable at launch. On PPP localization, converging benchmarks say: India/SEA should be priced 50-80% below US, Turkey ~30-55% below, Brazil/Mexico ~40-62% below, most of Western Europe roughly at/slightly below US; evidence that this works includes Adapty's finding that locale tests produce the single highest LTV uplift of any paywall test type (62.3%), RevenueCat case studies (Flo making Brazil its #3 market, a 60% price cut yielding 3x more payers, +38% conversion in SEA), and RevenueCat 2026 geography data showing NA vs India/SEA monetization gaps of 2.3x per payer and 5x per install — i.e., low prices in emerging markets buy volume, not ARPU. For retention, Apple now offers a full toolkit: win-back offers (iOS 18+, surfaced by Apple itself in the App Store and Settings), promotional offers (up to 10 active per subscription SKU), offer codes (up to 1M redemptions/app/quarter, max 6-month one-time codes), and Billing Grace Period (3/16/28 days, revenue uninterrupted if recovered) on top of up-to-60-day billing retry; billing failures cause ~14% of App Store cancellations, and dunning/retry programs recover 30-70% of involuntary churn, mostly within 72 hours.

### Факты
- The App Store offers 900 price points per app, ranging from $0.29 to $10,000 (the 100 highest points only upon request), with increments of $0.10 up to $10 and $0.50 between $10-$50, plus non-.99 rounding conventions (X.00, X.90); subscriptions got all 900 points first (Dec 2022), paid apps/IAP in spring 2023. [Apple Newsroom, 2022 (still current 2026) | https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2022/12/apple-announces-biggest-upgrade-to-app-store-pricing-adding-700-new-price-points/]
- Pricing is per-storefront: you choose 1 of 175 storefronts (~44 currencies) as the base; Apple auto-generates equalized prices for the other 174 and periodically auto-updates them as FX/taxes change, but the base-storefront price is never auto-adjusted, and any storefront you price manually is permanently excluded from automatic adjustments. [Apple Developer — App Store Connect Help, 'Set a price', 2025-2026 | https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-pricing/set-a-price/]
- Apple is merchant of record for App Store IAP/subscriptions in nearly all storefronts: it calculates, collects and remits VAT/GST based on the buyer's location; developer proceeds = customer price minus applicable taxes, then minus Apple's commission — the developer never collects or remits VAT for IAP (but WOULD become responsible for VAT if selling via external web checkout). [Apple Developer — 'Understanding taxes' + Afternoon blog sales-tax guide, 2025-2026 | https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/making-payments-to-apple/understanding-taxes/]
- Apple's automatic equalization is FX+tax based, NOT purchasing-power based: for a $19.99 US base price (April 2026 data), auto-converted vs typical manually-localized prices are India ₹1,899 vs ₹659 (-65%), Indonesia Rp345,000 vs Rp121,000 (-65%), Brazil R$129.90 vs R$49.50 (-62%), Mexico MX$399 vs MX$174 (-56%), Turkey ₺999.99 vs ₺449 (-55%), UK -25%, Germany -24%. [PricePush — App Store Pricing by Country, 2026 (April, updated July) | https://pricepush.app/blog/app-store-pricing-by-country]
- App Store Small Business Program: 15% commission (instead of 30%) on all paid apps and IAP including year-1 subscriptions, for developers with under $1M USD in prior-calendar-year proceeds across all apps; requires enrollment; exceeding $1M mid-year moves future sales to standard rate, and you can re-qualify the year after falling back under $1M. [Apple Developer — Small Business Program, 2025-2026 | https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/]
- Independent of the Small Business Program, auto-renewable subscription commission drops from 30% to 15% for any subscriber retained past one year of accumulated paid service — so a sub-$1M indie pays 15% from day one via SBP, and would keep 15% on long-retained subscribers even after outgrowing SBP. [RevenueCat — The 15% App Store Fee guide, 2026 | https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/engineering/small-business-program]

### Выводы для локалей/цен
For locale selection: keep the US/EN storefronts as the ARPU core (NA Year-1 value per payer $32, Day-60 RPI $0.55), and treat the already-localized emerging locales (India, Indonesia, Brazil, Thailand + pt-PT/ru) as volume markets where ads carry most of the monetization and the subscription is priced for accessibility — expect roughly 2.3x lower value per payer and 5x lower revenue per install in India/SEA even with correct pricing. For pricing mechanics: set the US as the base storefront, then manually override every emerging-market storefront (manual prices are never auto-adjusted by Apple, so revisit them 1-2x/year for FX drift, especially Turkey/Argentina-class currencies): use ~0.2-0.5x US for India/Indonesia/Thailand/Vietnam/Philippines (50-80% cheaper, e.g. $19.99/yr US -> ~₹659-class points), ~0.4-0.45x for Brazil/Mexico, ~0.45-0.7x for Turkey, ~0.85-0.95x for Western Europe/Canada/Australia; Apple's 900 price points down to $0.29 and non-.99 endings make clean local charm prices possible (₹X99, Rp X9,000). All prices are VAT-inclusive with Apple as merchant of record — quote gross prices and remember proceeds are net of local VAT (so a nominally 'equal' EU price nets ~19-21% less than a US price before the 15% cut). Budget LTV at 85% of net-of-VAT price (SBP 15%); ignore Epic/DMA at launch. For the subscription design: enable the 16- or 28-day Billing Grace Period from day one (billing issues are ~14% of App Store cancellations and recovery is cheap revenue), plan win-back offers (iOS 18+) and promotional offers for lapsed users — Maze Arrows' iOS 16+ floor means win-back offers reach only iOS 18+ devices, worth noting — and use offer codes (up to 1M redemptions/quarter) for launch marketing. Given 35% of annual-plan cancellations happen in Month 1 and long trials convert ~70% better than short ones, favor a 1-2 week trial on the annual plan and anchor the annual price against the monthly equivalent (that anchoring lifted trial starts +30% and yearly take rate +10% in LatAm).

## Направление: sub-design

### Резюме
Subscriptions do work in casual/puzzle games, but as a complement to ads, not a replacement: gaming is the fastest subscription category to first revenue (median 32 days to $1K MRR, 9% of new gaming apps reach $10K MRR) yet has the lowest realized LTV per payer ($11.22 median Y1) and the lowest download-to-paid conversion (1.0% D35), so ads must remain the primary monetization for the ~99% who never pay. The dominant, data-backed design for a game subscription in 2025-2026 is: a WEEKLY plan (82% of all gaming subscriptions sold are weekly; weekly+trial is the highest-12-month-LTV configuration at $49.27) with a short 3-4 day free trial (73-96% of gaming trials are ≤4 days; gaming trial-to-paid median is 25%), surfaced in a multi-page onboarding paywall (converts 37% better than single-page per Superwall's 40M-open study) and re-shown at natural feature-gate moments (after level completion, on hint exhaustion). Perk stack that converts in casual puzzle: remove forced ads (29% of game spenders say they'd pay for ad-free), unlimited/daily hints, exclusive content/themes, daily rewards — while KEEPING rewarded video available (or auto-granting its rewards) for subscribers, which is the Unity/Adapty industry consensus and avoids LTV loss. Churn reality: gaming first-renewal medians are 51% weekly / 53% monthly / 26% annual, with retention converging to 74-91% by the third weekly renewal, so the whole battle is weeks 1-3. Canonical comps are cautionary: Easybrain ($309M FY23/24 revenue, sold for $1.2B) monetizes predominantly via advertising and its flagship Sudoku.com sells a one-time $14.99 "No Ads" IAP on iOS rather than a subscription, while GameHouse runs a full unlimited-access subscription platform for casual games — both models coexist. Biggest optimization lever found: locale/price experiments (62.3% median LTV uplift, prices vary 4x between countries), directly relevant to Maze Arrows' 8-locale setup.

### Выводы для локалей/цен
For Maze Arrows' hybrid model, subscription revenue should be planned around high-ARPU storefronts (US/CA, UK/AU within 'en', pt-PT/Europe, and developed APAC) where trial-to-paid conversion is highest, while en-IN/hi (India), id (Indonesia) and th (Thailand) should be treated as ads-first markets: Adapty places India & Southeast Asia at the bottom of trial-to-paid conversion globally, so there the subscription mainly serves the small premium minority and needs steep regional pricing (global prices vary 4x between countries; Europe prices 29-39% above North America). Design the subscription weekly-first everywhere — weekly is 82% of gaming subscription sales and matches price-sensitive markets (a low absolute weekly price like $0.99-1.49-equivalent in India/Indonesia/Thailand converts where a $24.99 annual cannot) — with a 3-day trial on the weekly SKU and an annual SKU as anchor. Russia (ru) is a special case: App Store payments remain restricted, so treat ru as an ads-only locale for revenue planning. The single highest-leverage post-launch activity is locale-targeted price testing (median 62.3% LTV uplift, the top-performing test type in RevenueCat's 2026 data), which argues for launching in all 8 existing locales and immediately A/B testing regional price tiers rather than agonizing over launch pricing. Perk design that travels across locales: remove forced interstitials + unlimited/daily hints + exclusive themes/Photo Maze priority + daily reward, while keeping rewarded-video rewards auto-granted to subscribers; expect ~1% of downloads to ever pay, so interstitial/rewarded eCPM economics (strongest in US/UK/AU/CA, weakest in India/Indonesia) should drive the final storefront prioritization more than subscription potential."

## Направление: l10n-roi

### Резюме
Research across ASO vendors (AppTweak, SplitMetrics, Phiture, aso.dev/Asodesk), localization vendors (LocalizeDirect), market analysts (Sensor Tower, AppMagic, Newzoo, Statcounter) and monetization platforms (Appodeal, RevenueCat, Adapty) yields a clear picture. Listing-only localization (screenshots/keywords/description) is the highest-ROI first step: measured uplifts run +15-40% conversion in non-English markets (SplitMetrics: +36% in Japan for a puzzle game; +33% in China), while full in-game localization adds the classic Distimo-measured +128% downloads/+26% revenue effect on iPhone. For this iOS-only, ads+subscription puzzle game, Japanese is the standout next language: Japan combines 61% iOS share, the world's highest per-capita game spend (~$160/9 months), puzzle at 33.2% of all game downloads, the highest median annual subscription price ($54.59), and proof that Western casual puzzles (Block Blast!, Color Block Jam) top Japanese download charts. German is second (Germany is the #5 game market at $2.6B, fastest-growing major market in 2025 at +5.1%, highest median monthly sub price $17.19, plus DACH's high eCPMs) despite only ~27% iOS share. Spanish (es + es-MX) is third mostly for its ASO leverage — es-MX metadata is indexed in the US storefront, roughly doubling US keyword space to ~194 characters — plus Spain/LatAm reach. Traditional Chinese (Taiwan #3 worldwide iOS rewarded eCPM at $15.62), French, Korean and Arabic (Saudi #2 iOS rewarded eCPM $17.54, but RTL effort) follow; Simplified Chinese is demoted because the mainland China App Store requires a game license (ISBN) that indies rarely obtain, leaving zh-Hans mainly as a US-storefront keyword play. Turkish, Italian, Polish, Vietnamese and Dutch are low priority on revenue-per-effort for an iOS-only hybrid-monetized title. A zero-cost immediate win: the US storefront already indexes ru and pt-BR (both shipped), so filling those locales' 100-char keyword fields with English/US-intent keywords expands US search coverage today.
