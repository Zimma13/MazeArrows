The board looks impossible—until one clever move opens the way.

Arrow Maze is a satisfying logic puzzle where every tap matters. An arrow can leave only when the route in front of it is clear. Read the grid, release the right pieces in the right sequence and watch the tangle disappear. Tap too soon and you may lose a life; think ahead and your combo keeps climbing.

HOW TO PLAY
• Follow the direction of every arrow.
• Tap only when its route is open.
• Remove key pieces first to free new paths.
• Clear the board, save your lives and earn up to three stars.
