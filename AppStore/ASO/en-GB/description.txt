Every arrow has an exit. Can you uncover the sequence that clears the entire board?

Arrow Maze is a crisp logic puzzle where each tap changes what becomes possible next. An arrow can move only when its route is open. Read the grid, release the right piece and turn a tangled maze into a wonderfully satisfying clear. Move too quickly and you may lose a life; plan carefully and your combo will keep growing.

HOW TO PLAY
• Check the direction of every arrow.
• Tap only when the route ahead is open.
• Remove the right pieces first to reveal new paths.
• Clear the board, protect your lives and earn up to three stars.

PLENTY TO MASTER
• Explore 260 levels across eight worlds. In Signal Gates, each key opens every gate with its symbol.
• Tackle a fresh Daily Puzzle, maintain your streak and finish three Daily Quests.
• Play as many Zen boards as you like, at your own pace.
• Turn photos into playable mazes and adjust their detail and silhouettes.
• Build combos, unlock achievements and compare scores with Game Center.
• Pick a visual theme that suits the way you play.

The rules take seconds to learn, but the later boards demand patience, pattern recognition and smart sequencing. Play one quick level or settle in for a longer puzzle session.
