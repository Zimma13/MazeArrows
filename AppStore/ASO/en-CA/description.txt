One smart move can open the entire board. Can you spot it?

Arrow Maze is a polished path puzzle built around simple rules and meaningful choices. Every arrow needs a clear route before it can leave. Study the grid, free the right pieces in the right order, and watch a crowded maze fall into place. A rushed tap may cost a life; a well-planned chain turns into a satisfying combo.

HOW TO PLAY
• Read the direction of every arrow.
• Tap a piece only when the path ahead is open.
• Clear key arrows first to create new routes.
• Finish the board, protect your lives, and earn up to three stars.

KEEP EXPLORING
• Take on 240 levels spread across seven distinct worlds.
• Play a new Daily Puzzle, grow your streak, and complete three Daily Quests.
• Relax with gentler Zen sessions.
• With Premium, transform a selected photo into a custom playable maze.
• Earn achievements and compare your progress through Game Center.

Arrow Maze is easy to pick up and increasingly challenging to master. It fits a quick break, a daily routine, or an evening of focused problem-solving.
