Every arrow has a way out. Can you find the order that clears the whole board?

Arrow Maze is a clean, strategic puzzle where every tap matters. An arrow can leave only when its path is open. Read the grid, free the right route, and turn a crowded maze into a satisfying clear. Rush and you may lose a life; plan well and your combo keeps climbing.

HOW TO PLAY
• Study the direction of every arrow.
• Tap a piece only when its route is clear.
• Remove the right arrows first to open new paths.
• Clear the board, protect your lives, and earn up to three stars.

MORE THAN ONE WAY TO PLAY
• Journey through 240 levels across seven evolving worlds.
• Return for a new Daily Puzzle, keep your streak, and complete three Daily Quests.
• Slow down with peaceful Zen sessions.
• With Premium, turn a photo into a playable maze with adjustable detail and silhouette controls.
• Build combos, collect achievements, and compare scores through Game Center.
• Choose a visual theme that makes the board feel like yours.

Arrow Maze starts simple, then grows into a layered test of planning, pattern recognition, and restraint. Levels are quick to understand, rewarding to replay, and ideal for both short breaks and long puzzle sessions.
