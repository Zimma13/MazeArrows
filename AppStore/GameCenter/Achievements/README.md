# Maze Arrows achievement icon set

Final App Store Connect upload assets. All PNG files numbered `01` through `15`
are 1024×1024 px, opaque sRGB images.

## Shared prompt direction

- Deep midnight-indigo full-bleed square with a subtle maze pattern.
- Centered circular medal with a restrained warm-gold rim.
- Premium geometric 3D-vector enamel and softly beveled glass materials.
- Maze Arrows palette: cyan `#5AF0FF`, magenta `#FF6BE8`, mint `#56F5B2`,
  violet `#1B1340`, navy `#07061A`.
- Front-facing composition, crisp silhouette, generous safe margins, no text,
  numbers, logos, watermark, transparent background, or unnecessary detail.

## Central emblems

1. Cyan maze route ending at a magenta victory flag.
2. Mint meadow leaf, cyan route, and magenta flower bud.
3. Mint evergreen tree with a winding cyan route.
4. Violet mountain peaks with a route to a magenta summit flag.
5. Folded map with an interconnected maze route and destination gem.
6. Exactly three gold stars.
7. Closed cyan eye crossed by a magenta beam.
8. Cyan lightning bolt fused with a maze route.
9. Neon flame with a maze route and five gold combo diamonds.
10. Mint laurel, three gold stars, and one meadow flower.
11. Calendar with three connected day gems.
12. Calendar checkmark with seven gold streak dots.
13. Gold trophy with maze-shaped handles and a magenta gem.
14. Mint checkmark seal connected to a cyan maze route.
15. Three gold coins with a cyan maze engraving.

The images were generated with the built-in image generation tool and then
normalized locally to the App Store Connect image specification.
