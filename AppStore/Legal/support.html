Quick help

How do I restore Premium?
Open Arrow Maze and use Restore Purchases on the Premium screen. Make sure the device is signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.
How do I cancel a subscription?
Open device Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Arrow Maze, then choose the available cancellation option. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.
Where can I request a refund?
App Store billing and refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
Why is Game Center unavailable?
Check your Game Center sign-in, network connection, Screen Time restrictions, and Apple service status. The core game remains playable without Game Center.
What happens to a photo used in Photo Maze?
The selected image is processed on your device and is not uploaded to the Developer.
How do I remove local game data?
Deleting Arrow Maze removes its local container, subject to Apple backup and restore behaviour. Game Center and purchase records are managed separately through your Apple Account.
