Quick help

What does the purchase of Arrow Maze 2.0 include?
The one-time purchase includes all 260 campaign levels across eight worlds, Daily and Zen play, unlimited hints and Photo Maze creations, and all six themes. Version 2.0 has no ads or in-app purchases. The App Store shows the price for your region.
How do I keep my old progress?
Update your existing installation through the App Store. Arrow Maze 2.0 uses your saved campaign progress, stars and settings on the device. The eighth world follows level 240. If you previously obtained the app, use the same Apple Account. For a missing download, check your App Store purchase history before deleting the app.
How do I restore Premium in version 1.x?
Open Arrow Maze and use Restore Purchases on the Premium screen. Make sure the device is signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.
I subscribed in version 1.x. Where can I check my next charge?
On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then open Subscriptions and select Arrow Maze. Apple shows your renewal or expiration status there. You can cancel an active subscription on that screen even after updating to 2.0. Updating or deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. Apple’s cancellation instructions.
Where can I request a refund?
App Store billing and refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
Why is Game Center unavailable?
Check your Game Center sign-in, network connection, Screen Time restrictions, and Apple service status. The core game remains playable without Game Center.
What happens to a photo used in Photo Maze?
The selected image is processed on your device and is not uploaded to the Developer.
How do I remove local game data?
Deleting Arrow Maze removes its local container, subject to Apple backup and restore behaviour. Game Center and purchase records are managed separately through your Apple Account.

Privacy requests

Read the Privacy Policy. To request access to or deletion of information that you sent directly to support, email support@kinapp.dev. Arrow Maze does not operate a gameplay account or server-side player profile in the current version.
