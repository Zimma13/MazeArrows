## 2. Information handled by the App

### Game progress and settings

Completed levels, stars, scores, achievements, streaks, virtual items, hints, settings, onboarding state, and similar gameplay information are stored locally in the App’s container on your device. We do not receive this information on a developer-operated server.

### Photo Maze

You choose a specific image using Apple’s system Photos Picker. The selected image is processed on your device to identify a silhouette, colours, and maze geometry. The original image is not uploaded to us and is released from the App’s working memory when it is no longer needed. The App does not request unrestricted access to your photo library.

### Local notifications

If you grant permission, the App schedules reminders on your device for daily puzzles. These are local notifications. We do not receive a push token or use a developer-operated push notification server in the current version.

### Game Center

When Game Center is available, the App may access your Game Center player identifier, display name, alias, and restriction status, and may submit scores and completed achievements to Apple. The App uses the restriction status on-device to choose an age-appropriate advertising treatment. The status itself is not sent to a developer-operated server; only the resulting treatment setting is supplied to Google’s consent and advertising SDKs on the device. A local synchronization record associated with the Game Center player identifier is stored on your device. Game Center is operated by Apple, and the game remains playable if Game Center is unavailable.

### Purchases

Subscriptions and purchase restoration use Apple StoreKit. The App receives product information and verified transaction and entitlement status needed to provide purchased features. Apple processes payment and billing information; we do not receive your full payment card or banking details.

### Diagnostics

Google Mobile Ads and its consent component may process non-user-related crash logs, performance information such as launch time, hang rate or energy use, and other diagnostic data to diagnose problems, improve the SDK, measure advertising, and support advertising features. The operating system may also create diagnostic or crash reports. If you choose to share diagnostics with Apple, Apple may make diagnostic information available under its own privacy terms and settings.

### Support

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, screenshots, and diagnostic details. Please do not send sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for your request.

- submit scores and achievements when Game Center is used;
- deliver reminders that you have permitted;
- deliver, limit the frequency of, measure, and protect advertising, and remember applicable consent choices;
- diagnose crashes and performance problems and improve the reliability of the advertising components;
- respond to support requests, investigate defects, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The free experience uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform to deliver interstitial and rewarded ads and to collect or remember consent choices where required. Depending on the device, region, consent status, and SDK operation, Google may receive or process:

- an IP address used to estimate coarse location;
- a device identifier, including an advertising identifier when access is lawfully available or another app- or developer-bounded identifier;
- advertising data, such as ads shown and interactions with them;
- product interaction data, such as app launches, taps, and video views; and
- crash, performance, and other diagnostic data.

These data may be used for third-party advertising, ad delivery and frequency capping, fraud prevention, aggregate reporting, advertising measurement, analytics, and diagnosing or improving the advertising components. Premium disables new Google ad requests and ad presentations while the entitlement is active.

The current version disables personalized advertising and Google publisher first-party ID, does not request access to the iOS advertising identifier through App Tracking Transparency, and is not configured by the Developer to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. Non-personalized ads may still use mobile identifiers for limited purposes such as frequency capping and aggregate reporting. We do not sell personal information.

## 6. Storage, retention, and deletion

- Local gameplay data remains in the App’s container until you reset applicable progress, erase the device, or delete the App, subject to Apple device backup and restore behaviour.
- A selected photo is held only as needed for the active Photo Maze workflow. Maze geometry may remain while you play the generated level, but the App does not persist or upload the original photo to us.
- Game Center and App Store purchase records are retained by Apple according to Apple’s policies and your Apple Account settings.
- Advertising, consent, and diagnostic data processed by Google is retained under Google’s policies and applicable consent or legal requirements. We do not operate a separate server-side advertising profile.
- Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain security and support records, and meet legal obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized where reasonably possible.

Because we do not operate an Arrow Maze account or gameplay server, we ordinarily do not hold a server-side gameplay profile to delete. You can remove local App data by deleting the App. You can manage Game Center, subscriptions, and Apple data through your Apple Account. To request deletion of information you sent directly to support, email [{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}](mailto:{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}).

## 7. Your choices and rights

You can decline or revoke notification permission in device Settings, stop using Photo Maze, sign out of Game Center through Apple settings, and manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account. Where required, you can review or change advertising consent choices through **Settings → Advertising choices** in the App. Purchasing Premium prevents new ad requests and presentations while Premium is active. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to our handling of personal information and to lodge a complaint with a local data protection authority. Contact us to exercise an applicable right.

## 8. Children

Arrow Maze is a general-audience game and is not directed specifically to children. It does not require a developer-operated account and does not knowingly request a child’s name, contact information, precise location, photo, or other personal information. Photos selected for Photo Maze are processed on the device and are not uploaded to us. A parent or guardian should supervise use by anyone who cannot legally consent on their own and should configure the child’s Apple Account, Game Center, purchases, advertising consent, and device permissions appropriately.
