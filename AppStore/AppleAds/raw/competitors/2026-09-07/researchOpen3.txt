Fear not though, this puzzle is minimalistic and relaxing, not cluttered and stressful.

Levels start off with just a few arrows, and quickly work up to more and more, keeping things interesting. While the puzzle required you to use your logic skills and brain, it still manages to be relaxing and just the perfect level of challenging.

Easily toggle the music and haptic on or off depending on your mood, and don’t worry about tons of popups and events cluttering your game.
L55: ● The relaxing gameplay – Each level is short and sweet, meaning you can easily squeeze one in whenever you need a distraction. Last but not least boosters are available in case you can’t quite get past a tricky level.
