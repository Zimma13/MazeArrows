L52: ► FOLLOW THAT ARROW Get your mind and fingers set to untangle mazes of arrows in the fun take on a traditional maze escape game. Fear not though, this puzzle is minimalistic and relaxing, not cluttered and stressful.
L53: ► MASTER THE MAZE Colorful arrows twist and turn around each other, and it’s up to you to figure out which way they’re headed and help them escape from the puzzle grid, making way for others to be moved as well. Levels start off with just a few arrows, and quickly work up to more and more, keeping things interesting. While the puzzle required you to use your logic skills and brain, it still manages to be relaxing and just the perfect level of challenging.
L54: You’ll love: ● The minimalistic design – Black background, colorful arrows, and no crazy animations or over-the-top graphics – focus on the puzzle without worrying about what might pop up next. The bright arrows stand out well from the background making it easier to see how the maze all fits together, and adding a touch of whimsy to the game. Easily toggle the music and haptic on or off depending on your mood, and don’t worry about tons of popups and events cluttering your game.
L55: ● The relaxing gameplay – Each level is short and sweet, meaning you can easily squeeze one in whenever you need a distraction. Plus there’s no countdown clock to put pressure on you, so if you get stuck you can easily step away and come back to finish later when you’ve got the time and energy. Last but not least boosters are available in case you can’t quite get past a tricky level.
L56: ● The mental stimulation – While the puzzles might seem simple at first, you’ll find that they start getting challenging fast!
