Point Out is a clever directional puzzle where every move matters, designed for fans of brain and mind challenges. Your goal is simple: guide the right arrow through the maze and reach freedom in this smart escape experience. Rotate arrows, shift tiles, and plan ahead using pure logic and spatial thinking. Smooth flow and responsive tap controls make every decision feel precise and satisfying. With gradually increasing difficulty and thousands of handcrafted levels, Point Out stands among the best games for iq improvement, training, and problem solving. Each level works like a compact teaser, pushing you to think ahead and adapt. When a level gets tricky, helpful boosters are there to support you: Use Zoom to closely inspect complex paths, Hint to reveal a helpful move, Magic Wand to break through difficult situations, Rubber to erase mistakes, and Grid Scale to adjust the board for better visibility and planning. Game Features • Direction-based logic puzzles with evolving mechanics • Relaxing yet challenging brain training games experience • Thoughtful escape puzzle and shape escape level design • Offline-friendly play with no wifi games for free • Precision controls, helpful boosters, and a comfortable dark mode for extended play Point Out blends brain games, mind games, and smart interaction into a polished experience that rewards patience and clever thinking. Can you find the way out — or will your arrows go in circles?   • Wonderful way to occupy your time
    Jan 23
    Eilonwe

    I play plenty of mobile games. While some are idle games, I honestly prefer logic games. I don’t like pay-to-win pvp games at all. This game is awesome. The premise is simple, help all the arrows escape without hitting anything. It make use of spacial recognition and simple logic. You just have to be patient before clicking an arrow. So far, there’s no timer on any levels. Now, I will say (my only criticism) is that for some of the super difficult levels, the color of some of the very dark colors, are VERY DARK! I’m not color blond but it was wicked hard for me to see the which way the arrows might go out. I would suggest building something in the profile that allows gamers to correct for color blindness or a need to increase the contrast of color. Now, I realize this mayb”lower” the difficulty for some players. But honestly, even I accidentally click the wrong arrow and will spend lives and coins to replay levels for 3 stars. Another recommendation I would make is to allow players to scroll back to replay all levels. Right now, you can only scroll back to levels in your current section. And right now I’m on level 392. But, I have to say, I’m addicted to this game and I look forward to playing this daily.

  • They got it exactly right!
    Mar 7
    NekoTheSpook

    I have never been particularly interested in traffic jam type games, but I decided to give this one a try. I ended up getting fully immersed into the gameplay. It’s simple enough, move all the arrows off the screen without hitting any of the others. What makes this hit in all the right places is there’s NO timer. There’s no pop-up ad in the middle of your gameplay (it’s so irksome when you’re laser-focused on something and then BOOM, an ad shows up). Ads are limited to a banner at the bottom and between each game. No teams, no enemy to challenge, no distraction - just you unraveling a big knot of arrows. And you KNOW there’s an answer that will solve it, you just have to look. Got fat fingers and don’t know if you can touch the correct line? No problem, the screen easily pinches in or out without triggering an erroneous move. Still need help? Plenty of tools to erase arrows or give you grid lines, BUT EVERY PUZZLE CAN BE SOLVED WITHOUT THEM. It’s the absolute best ASMR game I’ve played in a long time. Devs, give yourselves a pat on the back and a cookie for doing a fantastic job!
